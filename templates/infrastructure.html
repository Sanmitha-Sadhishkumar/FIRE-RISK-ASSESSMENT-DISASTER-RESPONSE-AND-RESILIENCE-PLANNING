Assess Infrastructure in Disaster-Prone Areas

Infrastructure Suggestions

  • Install early-warning systems for seismic activities in critical infrastructure areas.
  • Use smart sensors to monitor building vibrations and alert authorities in real-time.
  • Develop modular housing units that can be rapidly deployed post-disaster.
  • Elevate critical infrastructure (like hospitals) in flood-prone regions.
  • Retrofit older buildings and heritage structures to withstand moderate earthquakes.
  • Implement automated power shut-off systems in high-risk electrical zones during tremors.
  • Create decentralized water treatment and storage units to ensure supply during emergencies.
  • Use fiber-reinforced concrete and shock-absorbing materials in key constructions.

Urban Planning Suggestions

  • Incorporate vertical gardens and green terraces to reduce urban heat and fire risk.
  • Design neighborhoods with multiple evacuation routes for all age groups.
  • Zone low-risk areas for high-density populations and essential services.
  • Introduce incentives for relocating industries from high-risk zones.
  • Use LiDAR and drone mapping for regular city-scale structural assessments.
  • Map and maintain clear emergency shelter locations accessible within a 15-minute walk.
  • Limit building heights and density in zones with poor soil stability.
  • Digitally twin the city for simulated emergency preparedness and planning.
